# Brand Identity

## Positioning

**Trust-first plastic surgery research for Georgia** — not a clinic, not a beauty brand, not influencer marketing.

| Name | Notes |

**Recommendation for MVP:** MedPlatform until legal/trademark review.

## Tone of voice

- Calm, direct, respectful
- Evidence-oriented (“patients reported”, “verify independently”)
- Never hype outcomes or guarantee results
- Acknowledge uncertainty and risk

## Visual direction

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Background | Ivory, white, soft beige |
| Accent | Soft blue / muted teal |
| Typography | Playfair Display (headlines) + IBM Plex Sans (UI) + Noto Sans Georgian |
| Layout | Editorial spacing, cinematic hero, clean cards |
| Motion | Subtle Framer Motion reveals; no flashy transitions |

## Logo direction

- Wordmark + simple geometric mark (e.g. balanced scales, open book, or abstract “research lens”)
- Monochrome ink + single accent color
- Avoid scalpel clichés and overly clinical crosses

## Avoid

- Beauty salon / spa aesthetics
- Pink, gold, glitter, “luxury surgery” gradients
- Before/after glam grids as hero imagery
- Aggressive CTAs (“Book now”, “Transform your life”)

## Inspiration references

- **RealSelf** — structured reviews and procedure taxonomy
- **Deschamps-Braly** — premium editorial spacing (not copied layout)
- **AEDIT** — modern procedure discovery
- **Reddit** — community voting and thread logic

## Sponsored content labeling (future)

Always use: **“Sponsored”** or **“Paid placement”** — distinct card border, no score influence.
